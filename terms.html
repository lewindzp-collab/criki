1. What Criki is

Criki is a free, personal project that indexes publicly available IPTV stream links and sports fixtures, and tries to pair them together. It is not a broadcaster, is not affiliated with any sports league or rights holder, and does not host, upload, or store any video content itself. All streams are third-party links sourced from iptv-org/iptv, and fixture data comes from TheSportsDB.
