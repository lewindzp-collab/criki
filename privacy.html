Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Short version: Criki has no server, no account system, and no analytics. Nothing you do on this site is sent anywhere or seen by anyone. The only "storage" involved is your own browser holding onto your favorites and current tab, and it never leaves your device.

1. No accounts, no backend

Criki doesn't have a login, a database, or a server that processes your data. It's a static site: your browser loads the page, then fetches channel and fixture data directly from third-party sources (see below). There is nothing on Criki's side collecting or storing information about you.

2. What's stored in your browser

Two small, local-only mechanisms are used, both readable only by this site in your own browser:

  • localStorage — your favorited channels, so they're still there next time you visit this browser.
  • sessionStorage — which tab you were on and your scroll position, so navigating to a channel and back doesn't reset the page. Cleared automatically when you close the tab.

Neither is transmitted to Criki or anyone else — it's local browser storage, not a cookie sent with requests. Clearing your browser's site data for Criki removes all of it.

3. Third-party data sources

Criki's content comes from external, independent sources, fetched directly by your browser:

  • iptv-org/iptv and its companion API — channel listings, stream links, and country data.
  • TheSportsDB — match fixtures, team names, and logos.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — for typefaces and the video-playback library (hls.js).

Each of these is a separate service with its own infrastructure, and your browser connects to them directly (their standard request logs, e.g. IP address and request time, are outside Criki's control — see each provider's own policy for details).

4. The streams themselves

When you play a channel, your browser connects directly to that stream's own third-party host to fetch the video. Criki doesn't proxy, intercept, or log that connection — but the stream's host may have its own logging or tracking, entirely outside Criki's knowledge or control. Treat third-party streams the same way you would any other external site you click through to.

5. No analytics, no ads, no tracking scripts

Criki doesn't run any analytics platform, ad network, or tracking pixel. There's no mechanism here for building a profile of who's visiting or what they're watching.

6. Changes to this policy

If what's stored or fetched ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub or through saikot.dev.
